# Service Level Agreement: Response Times

Response time targets are based on ticket priority level assigned at the time of submission:

- Critical: First response within 1 hour. Assigned to a senior support engineer immediately.
  Reserved for full service outages, data breaches, or complete loss of business function.
- High: First response within 4 hours. Assigned to a specialist within the relevant queue.
  Reserved for significant disruption affecting multiple users or core business processes.
- Medium: First response within 1 business day. Standard support queue assignment.
  Reserved for partial disruptions, workarounds available, single-user issues.
- Low: First response within 3 business days. General queue assignment.
  Reserved for informational requests, minor inconveniences, and feature inquiries.

SLA timers pause outside business hours (9am-6pm local time, Monday to Friday).
