# General Support Process

When a support ticket is submitted, it is automatically classified by category and 
priority. Routine tickets receive an AI-assisted draft response within the SLA window, 
grounded in resolved similar tickets or relevant policy documents. Customers are notified 
by email when their ticket receives a response.

If the automated response does not resolve the issue, customers can reply to the ticket 
to continue the conversation or request escalation to a human agent. Tickets are 
automatically closed after 7 days of inactivity. Customers can reopen a closed ticket 
within 30 days by replying to the closure notification.
