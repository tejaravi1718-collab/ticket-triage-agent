# Billing Disputes Policy

Customers who identify incorrect charges on their account should raise a billing dispute 
within 60 days of the charge date. Disputes raised after this window may not be eligible 
for review. To raise a dispute, customers must provide their account ID, the charge date, 
and a description of the discrepancy.

Duplicate charges are automatically refunded within 3-5 business days once verified. 
For disputed subscription renewals, the account will be temporarily suspended from 
further billing while the dispute is under review. Resolution typically takes 7-10 
business days.
