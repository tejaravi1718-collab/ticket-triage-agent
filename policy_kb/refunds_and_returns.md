# Refunds and Returns Policy

Customers are eligible for a full refund within 30 days of purchase, provided the product 
or service has not been fully consumed or activated. Refund requests must be submitted 
through the support portal with a valid order ID and reason for return. Processing takes 
5-7 business days after approval.

For digital products and software licenses, refunds are only issued if the product is 
reported non-functional within 7 days of purchase and the issue cannot be resolved by 
the support team. Partially used subscriptions are refunded on a pro-rata basis.
